# Research Interest Board

A sourced, presentable board on:

- AI news coverage, Oct 2023 – Sep 2026 (monthly volume from the GDELT Project)
- Landmark AI headlines and the past month (September 2026)
- AI in legal systems: sanctions, court rules, copyright, regulation, legal-AI industry
- Vagueness in law and how well LLMs interpret legal language
- Data privacy in legal systems (with Harvard Data Science Review sources)
- Credited video clips and published projections

Every item links to its source. It's a single static page (`index.html`) with no build step.
